What we wrench on

Built for the daily grind.

From a check-engine light to a truck that won't turn over, we handle the work that keeps Michigan moving.

01

Diesel Diagnostics

Computer scan tools and hands-on testing to find the real cause of codes, low power, smoke, hard starts and no-starts.

02

Fuel & Injection

Injectors, high-pressure pumps, lift pumps, filters and fuel delivery problems diagnosed and repaired.

03

Turbochargers

Boost leaks, worn turbos, actuators and intercooler issues — tracked down and fixed so you get your power back.

04

Emissions Systems

DPF, EGR, DEF and SCR system diagnosis and repair to keep your rig running clean and out of limp mode.

05

Brakes & Suspension

Brakes, steering, front-end and suspension work for heavy trucks that tow, haul and plow.

06

Maintenance & Auto

Oil service, fluids, cooling, electrical and general repair on diesels and gas vehicles alike.
